# Hours by Week

Add a screenshot of your timesheet and see the hours for each Sunday–Saturday week, plus an hours-and-minutes calculator.

Open it: https://jack-fortune.github.io/hours-by-week/

- Screenshots are read on your device (Tesseract OCR). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Shifts are saved in your browser only.
- On iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
